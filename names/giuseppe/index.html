Giuseppe

The Yousef who brought the pasta.

Meaning

Giuseppe is the Italian form of Joseph, from Latin Ioseph. It has been the most common male first name in Italy for generations.

The name of a nation

Giuseppe is the most common first name among Italian men, helped by the deep popularity of Saint Joseph. Saint Joseph's Day on March 19 brings zeppole and sfinci pastries in Naples and Sicily, and is Father's Day across Italy.

Nicknames

Beppe, Peppe, Peppino, Pino and Geppetto. Yes, Geppetto, the carpenter in Pinocchio, is simply a nickname for Giuseppe, which makes him a member of the extended Yousef family.

Famous Giuseppes

Giuseppe Garibaldi helped unify Italy in the 19th century, and Giuseppe Verdi wrote some of the most performed operas in the world.
