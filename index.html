Fun Yousef Facts

See all the facts →

12

Surah Yusuf, the 12th chapter of the Quran, tells Joseph's story almost from start to finish, which makes it unusual among the chapters.

P.P.

A popular folk explanation says Spanish "Pepe" comes from Pater Putativus, the title for Saint Joseph, abbreviated P.P. Linguists are skeptical. We are not.

19/3

March 19 is Saint Joseph's Day, celebrated as Father's Day in Italy, Spain, and Portugal. We propose it also become International Yousef Day.

7+7

Joseph's plan of seven years of saving for seven years of famine is arguably the first recorded emergency fund. Every Yousef is a financial planner at heart.
