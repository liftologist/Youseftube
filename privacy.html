Last updated October 29, 2026.

YousefTube is a fan and satire site. We collect your email address only if you subscribe to the newsletter, and use it only to send that newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Newsletter. Your email is stored securely with our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and used only to send the newsletter.

This site may show ads from Google and other ad partners. These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site, such as pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location (country, region and city, estimated from your IP address). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We do not sell personal information.
